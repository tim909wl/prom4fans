What we don't do, and what you should know before applying

A management agency in this space only works if you know exactly what you're getting into. Here's what we commit to.

We don't guarantee income

Any agency promising a specific monthly income figure is not being honest with you. Earnings depend on your content, your audience and the market. We'll share realistic ranges based on comparable creators, not promises.

You control your content
  • You decide what you post and how explicit it is, if at all — some creators on subscription platforms post no explicit content.
  • We never post on your behalf without sign-off.
  • You can pause or stop at any time; our contract has no lock-in beyond a notice period.
Our fee is transparent

We work on an agreed percentage of your subscription revenue, laid out in writing before you start — no hidden costs, no charges for "growth packages" on top.

Confidentiality

Your identity, your real name and your application details are kept confidential and are never shared or posted publicly by us.
